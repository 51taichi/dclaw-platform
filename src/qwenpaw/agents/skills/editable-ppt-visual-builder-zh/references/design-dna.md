# Commercial Design DNA Reference

Use this reference before imagegen prompting. Pick or combine design DNA archetypes by business purpose, audience, industry credibility, editability, and deck-wide scalability. Do not copy a specific template, brand, marketplace listing, or designer style.

## Selection Method

For each deck, shortlist 3-5 DNA candidates, score them, and keep 2-3 for imagegen exploration. Prefer concrete visual drivers over style adjectives.

For user-facing questions and selectable options, localize DNA names into the user's language. For Chinese users, use Chinese names as the primary option labels and keep English names only as optional internal slugs.

Good DNA statements:

- "战略 AI 指挥中心（AI Command Center）：模块化系统拓扑、克制发光、锐利标签、高管级深色画布。"
- "高级产品叙事（Premium Product Narrative）：电影感主视觉、宽松留白、超大主张、精准产品标注。"

Weak DNA statements:

- "Modern blue technology style."
- "High-end business template."
- "Make it trendy and clean."

## DNA Archetypes

### 战略咨询编辑风（Strategic Consulting Editorial）

- Fits: executive briefings, transformation plans, enterprise service proposals, strategy reports.
- Visual drivers: strong typography, structured grids, restrained palette, sharp dividers, evidence-led pages.
- Strong pages: thesis, framework, roadmap, executive summary, recommendations.
- Asset needs: subtle paper or editorial texture, occasional portrait/product image, minimal icons.
- Risk: can become plain consulting if there is no focal point or strong type scale.

### AI 指挥中心（AI Command Center）

- Fits: AI platforms, operating systems, enterprise automation, technical service systems.
- Visual drivers: dark control-room canvas, modular topology, precise labels, data-field texture, restrained glow.
- Strong pages: cover, architecture, service system, dashboard, roadmap.
- Asset needs: cover background, platform metaphor, subtle data texture.
- Risk: can become generic cyber-tech if fake UI and glow dominate.

### 高级产品叙事（Premium Product Narrative）

- Fits: product launches, high-value services, brand/product introductions, founder-led offers.
- Visual drivers: cinematic full-bleed imagery, spacious composition, oversized claim, minimal supporting copy.
- Strong pages: cover, product promise, key benefits, proof, closing ask.
- Asset needs: hero background, product metaphor, closing visual.
- Risk: can look like a poster if editable proof and structure are weak.

### 高管数据证明（Data Proof Executive）

- Fits: business cases, investor updates, performance reviews, ROI stories, proof-heavy sales decks.
- Visual drivers: oversized metric, clean chart grammar, annotation callouts, proof strips, quiet surfaces.
- Strong pages: KPI dashboard, before/after, case proof, financial narrative.
- Asset needs: minimal; mostly native charts and data components.
- Risk: can become a report page if hierarchy is not dramatic enough.

### 品牌宣言（Brand Manifesto）

- Fits: brand strategy, cultural narrative, public launch, team alignment, mission decks.
- Visual drivers: bold type-led pages, emotional imagery, contrast blocks, declarative statements.
- Strong pages: opening, beliefs, principles, movement/vision, closing.
- Asset needs: photographic or abstract emotional anchors, texture, signature marks.
- Risk: can feel hollow if claims are generic or proof is missing.

### 技术蓝图（Technical Blueprint）

- Fits: platform architecture, implementation plans, systems integration, product infrastructure.
- Visual drivers: blueprint grid, native diagrams, precise connectors, layered modules, restrained labels.
- Strong pages: architecture, process, operating model, roadmap, roles.
- Asset needs: light texture, diagram accents, technical icons.
- Risk: can become too dense or engineer-only; add executive summaries.

### 奢华极简刊物（Luxury Minimal Publication）

- Fits: premium personal introductions, boutique services, high-trust consulting, polished proposals.
- Visual drivers: serif or refined typography, generous whitespace, editorial image crops, quiet material texture.
- Strong pages: cover, profile, philosophy, selected proof, closing.
- Asset needs: editorial portrait/image, paper grain, refined dividers.
- Risk: can look under-designed if the topic needs energy or technical credibility.

### 工作坊行动手册（Workshop Playbook）

- Fits: training, enablement, internal rollout, operational handbooks, process education.
- Visual drivers: clear sequence, friendly frameworks, worksheets, step markers, practical diagrams.
- Strong pages: agenda, method, exercises, process, checklist, recap.
- Asset needs: minimal; native shapes and icons usually carry it.
- Risk: can look too casual for executive buyers.

### 投资增长势能（Investment Momentum）

- Fits: fundraising, growth stories, market expansion, strategic partnership decks.
- Visual drivers: upward movement, market scale, bold numbers, timeline momentum, decisive contrast.
- Strong pages: market, traction, strategy, financial outlook, ask.
- Asset needs: market/scale metaphor, motion-like arrows or horizon imagery.
- Risk: can overpromise if claims and numbers are not sourced.

### 案例证明杂志（Case Proof Magazine）

- Fits: case studies, customer proof, transformation stories, portfolios.
- Visual drivers: image strips, before/after contrast, quote pullouts, outcome metrics, editorial captions.
- Strong pages: case opener, challenge, solution, results, testimonial.
- Asset needs: real/provided imagery where possible; generated placeholders only when clearly conceptual.
- Risk: fake-looking images or invented evidence damage trust.
